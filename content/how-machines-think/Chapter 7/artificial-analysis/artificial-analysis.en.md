## Introduction

We have already seen that comparing LLMs is not just about looking at one score. Cost, speed, openness, and the benchmark behind each result also matter.

But **where can you see all of that together without jumping across many different sites?**

That is exactly what **Artificial Analysis** provides: a website designed to explore and compare AI models through metrics, rankings, charts, and evaluation pages.

This tool lets you move from a broad question like “which options exist?” to a much more concrete exploration of what performs better, what costs less, what responds faster, and which benchmarks support that comparison.

## What is Artificial Analysis?

{{% notice style="info" title="Artificial Analysis Essentials" %}}
**Artificial Analysis** is a model-comparison platform that brings together in one place:

- **Aggregated indexes**: for a quick comparison across options
- **Specific evaluations**: to drill down into particular benchmarks
- **Cost and pricing**: to understand how expensive a model is to use
- **Speed and latency**: to inspect practical performance
- **Openness**: to distinguish proprietary from open-weight models
- **Provider comparison**: in some cases, to compare how the same option behaves across providers

**How does it work?** Instead of giving you one static list, the site lets you navigate dashboards, filters, and methodology pages so you can understand what is being compared and why.
{{% /notice %}}

## The Artificial Analysis Comparison Platform

The official **Artificial Analysis** site (https://artificialanalysis.ai/) works like an interactive dashboard for exploring the current state of the model ecosystem.

Unlike a theoretical explanation or a fixed table in an article, here you can **see** updated comparisons, open specific evaluation pages, and inspect the methodology behind each metric.

The most useful pages to start with are:

- **Main site**: https://artificialanalysis.ai/
- **Intelligence Index methodology**: https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking
- **Evaluations hub**: https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/

*Mental picture: think of Artificial Analysis as an interactive map of the model landscape, where you can move from a broad overview to a highly specific comparison depending on what you want to understand.*

## What Can You See in Artificial Analysis?

{{% notice style="tip" title="Main Components" %}}

**1. Intelligence Index**
- Shows an aggregated comparison across many options
- Helps you orient yourself quickly before going deeper

**2. Specific Evaluations**
- Lets you open individual benchmarks and see what each one measures
- This helps separate knowledge, reasoning, factuality, or agentic ability

**3. Cost and Pricing**
- The platform includes views related to pricing and usage cost
- This helps you compare raw capability with practical efficiency

**4. Speed and Latency**
- You can also explore how fast an API responds or how many tokens per second it produces
- This matters when you care about real usage, not just abstract quality

**5. Openness and Transparency**
- The site distinguishes proprietary and open-weight options
- It also includes openness signals that many other rankings do not show
{{% /notice %}}

## From Overall Ranking to Specific Benchmark

One of the most useful ideas behind Artificial Analysis is that it does not force you to stay at only one level of reading.

You can start with a broad overview and then move toward more specific questions, such as:

- which option looks strongest overall;
- which option is better positioned on capability vs. cost;
- which specific evaluation explains a difference between two candidates.

This matters because an aggregate score never tells the full story. The platform is useful precisely because it lets you connect the **big picture** with the **methodological detail**.

{{% notice style="warning" title="Explore the Comparison Step by Step" %}}
On the site, try the following:

1. **Open the main dashboard** and observe the overall ecosystem view
2. **Enter the Intelligence Index** for a first aggregated comparison
3. **Open a specific evaluation** to understand what it is really measuring
4. **Inspect cost or speed charts** to see whether the strongest option is also practical
5. **Read the methodology** before drawing conclusions

**Note**: Because these comparisons change over time, the official site is the right place to inspect the latest state.
{{% /notice %}}
