The Artificial Brains series presents a comprehensive and humanistic approach to understanding artificial intelligence through three complementary volumes. Each book addresses AI from a different but interconnected perspective, creating a complete educational ecosystem that prepares readers not just to use AI, but to understand, question, and shape its role in society.

{{ if eq $lang "es" }}Por qué tres libros{{ else }}Why Three Books{{ end }}

{{ if eq $lang "es" }}

Cada libro se centra en un aspecto fundamental de la IA, pero juntos forman una comprensión completa:

  • Libro 1 te enseña cómo las máquinas aprenden de ejemplos etiquetados
  • Libro 2 explora cómo descubren patrones por sí mismas y aprenden mediante prueba y error
  • Libro 3 revela cómo perciben y generan contenido multimodal

Puedes leer cada libro de forma independiente, pero juntos ofrecen una visión completa del panorama de la IA moderna.

{{ else }}

Each book focuses on a fundamental aspect of AI, but together they form a complete understanding:

  • Book 1 teaches you how machines learn from labeled examples
  • Book 2 explores how they discover patterns on their own and learn through trial and error
  • Book 3 reveals how they perceive and generate multimodal content

You can read each book independently, but together they provide a complete view of the modern AI landscape.
